# Aangan Studio: Services Summary (from knowledge/services.md)

## Does
End-to-end interior design **with execution** (own contractor/vendor network) for:
- **Residential**: apartments, independent houses, villas
  - Full home (2BHK onwards)
  - Partial home (a full floor, or 2+ rooms)
  - Single room (bedroom or living room), complete redesign incl. materials, furniture, execution
- **Small commercial**: offices, clinics, studios, up to ~3,000 sq ft (workstations, cabins, reception, common areas)

Covers: space planning, material selection (flooring, walls, ceilings), custom and sourced furniture,
lighting, kitchens and wardrobes, execution supervision. Incorporates Vastu into designs.
Rented apartments are fine without structural changes (qualified.md).

## Does not do
- Architecture / structural work (moving walls, structure changes, structural permits)
- Decor/styling or advice only (colours, rearranging furniture); minimum is a room redesign with execution
- Standalone furniture sourcing
- Standalone Vastu consultation
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores, gyms
- Projects outside Pune / PCMC

## Areas served
- **Pune city**: Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Wakad, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar,
  Magarpatta, NIBM, Kondhwa, Undri, Shivane, Warje, Erandwane, Deccan, and adjoining areas
- **PCMC**: Pimpri, Chinchwad, Pimple Saudagar, Pimple Nilakh, Ravet, Hinjewadi
- **Not served**: Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai, other cities

## Process and timelines
Free consultation (often a site visit), then a 3–4 week design phase, then 8–16 weeks of execution
depending on size and site readiness. Can't start execution on anything that must be ready in under 6 weeks.
